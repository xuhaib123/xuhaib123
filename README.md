I am a Research Associate in Economics at the **Isaac Centre for Public Policy (ICPP), Ashoka University**, based in New Delhi, India. My work combines economic research, empirical analysis, and policy writing.

My research focuses on how monetary policy affects firms, how governments manage public finances, and how price shocks transmit through the economy. Previously, I was a **Teaching Fellow in Economics at Ashoka University**, supporting undergraduate and postgraduate teaching.

## Research

| Area | Focus |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Monetary transmission** | Firm-level heterogeneity in monetary policy transmission in India, using CMIE Prowess panel data. |
| **Public finance** | Bihar's fiscal landscape: deficit trends, revenue mobilisation, expenditure quality, and debt sustainability. |
| **Inflation and energy** | Crude oil price shocks and CPI inflation in India, using a Leontief input–output price framework. |

## Selected papers

**[Crude Oil Shock and CPI Inflation in India: Scenario Analysis Using the Leontief Price Model](https://icpp.ashoka.edu.in/policy/discussion-paper/crude-oil-shock-and-cpi-inflation-in-india)**  
With Vidushi Balakrishnan · ICPP Policy Discussion Paper · May 2026

Analyses how alternative crude oil price scenarios and domestic fuel-price pass-through policies affect consumer prices in India.

**Bihar's fiscal landscape** · Working paper

Examines fiscal deficits, revenue mobilisation, expenditure quality, and debt sustainability, with comparative benchmarks including Odisha.

**Firm-level heterogeneity in monetary policy transmission in India** · Ongoing research

Studies differences across firms in the transmission of monetary policy to investment.
